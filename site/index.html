Australian Parliament Registers of Interests

Every interest Australian federal MPs and senators disclosed in the Registers of Members' and Senators' Interests, transcribed item by item from the official statements: 50,936 items (shareholdings, trusts, property, directorships, gifts, sponsored travel, memberships, …), each joined with the member's party and a standardised entity (11,542 entities).

Explore the data in Datasette Lite

Datasette Lite runs in your browser: write SQL against the items, members, member_terms, documents and entities tables, filter and facet, and download results as CSV. Or download the SQLite database: disclosures_v2.db.

Caveats

The House statements were transcribed from PDFs by an LLM (precision and recall about 0.99 on a hand-checked sample). Expect a small share of items to be missed or misread, more on the scanned 43rd–45th parliament statements. Each item records its source document and page: check anything important against the original on aph.gov.au. The full field dictionary, method and known limitations are in the project repository.
