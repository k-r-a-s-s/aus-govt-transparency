About this dataset

Australian federal MPs and senators must declare their interests (shareholdings, trusts, property, directorships, gifts, sponsored travel, memberships and more) in the Registers of Members' and Senators' Interests. The registers are public but published as scanned or typed PDF statements, one per member per parliament. This dataset turns them into one row per declared item, with a link to the page each item came from.

The site describes what was declared. It does not estimate value or wealth, and it does not suggest that any declared interest is improper.
