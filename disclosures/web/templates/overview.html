How it was made, and how to cite it

House statements were transcribed from the PDFs by a language model and checked against a hand-checked gold set (precision {{ site.gold.precision }}, recall {{ site.gold.recall }}). Senate items come from the Parliament's structured Senate register. Expect roughly 1 to 2% of items to be missed or misread; items with medium or low transcription confidence are marked.
